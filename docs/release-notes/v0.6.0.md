# Marble Rush v0.6.0

HD backgrounds. The Toy Room now stands inside four painted walls, your picks from the decision page.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game and press Play. Behind the gate you should see the window seat wall straight ahead and the bunk bed with the yellow slide on your left.
2. Roll down Desk Drop. The room stays around you the whole way: no blank cream background.
3. On Rug Run, look ahead and right: the cubby shelves with the tin robot, the wooden train and the globe.
4. In the Block Tower spiral, look for the chalkboard with the race track doodle. On the finish straight you roll toward the bunk bed.
5. Check your marble and the track still stand out against the walls at every point. Tell me if the walls feel too soft or too strong.
6. Watch the frame rate on your phone. Tell me if it feels less smooth than 0.5.
7. Menu > Tracks > Kitchen. It looks the same as in 0.5. Its walls come next, once you like the Toy Room.

- Four painted walls in the Toy Room (your picks, 12:11 PM): Window seat, Cubby shelves, Bunk bed with a slide, Chalkboard.

## Judgment calls

- Which painting goes on which wall. Window seat on the far wall you face at the gate, so it is the first thing you see and it matches the sun. Cubby shelves beside Rug Run. Bunk bed at the end of the finish straight. Chalkboard behind the start desk.
- Haze strength. The first build looked washed out beside your picked drafts in the look check, so I halved the haze. It is one number per track. Say "stronger" or "softer" and I change it.
- Wall sharpness. 2048 px per wall is the studio limit for one texture (E3.3). The walls are sharp from most of the track and soft up close in the Block Tower. Two textures per wall fixes it for about 13 MB more (backlog idea 10).
- The floor stays as it was: it reads pale cream from above. The paintings have a wood strip along the bottom. A wood floor is backlog idea 9, because the brown track must still stand out on it.
- I cut a thin strip of painted floor off the bottom of each painting, stopping just under the lowest painted object, so no bin or bed leg is clipped.
- The Kitchen keeps its plain walls in this release (one area first, G4.13).
- The 12 drafts cost about $0.40 and the four final paintings $0.96 on your Google project.
- I cloned game-signing to sign the APK without asking first. G1.7 allows it for an APK build and every release needs one. Tell me if you want a yes each time.
- Fresh review (G7.14): one must fix (run the save store check, done) and two should fixes (the version line in `project.godot` and a stronger wall check in the race test, both done). Its notes are in the backlog under Later.
