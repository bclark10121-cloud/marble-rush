# Marble Rush v0.3.0

The first look and sound pass, plus your fix from the first run. Real music, a title font, a time trial against your own ghost, stars and name tags over the computer marbles.

## Five minute test

1. Play. The start screen plays "Quirky Dog" and the title is in the new round font.
2. Race. Watch the first two seconds after the gate drops: the marbles stay on the track (your first-run bug). The race plays "Hyperfun".
3. Look for the small names over the computer marbles near you.
4. Finish. The results show stars: finish, top three and under a minute.
5. Results > Time trial. Your marble races alone. A see-through ghost of your best run rolls beside you. The line under the time says how far ahead or behind the ghost you are.
6. Beat the ghost. Your new run becomes the ghost.
7. Open the decision page and make your picks (link in the reply). Your picks replace the music and font I put in.

## New in this version

- Fixed: at the start, the marbles rolled inside the desk for about two seconds (BC's first run). The desk was a flat box level with the gate, and the track slopes down from the gate. The desk now sits under the lowest point of the track above it. A new test (CLEAR) fails if any prop box ever covers the track.
- Music from Patch.gg: "Quirky Dog" on the start screen, "Hyperfun" in races. Both Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0. Picks are provisional until BC chooses on the decision page.
- Title font Fredoka on the start screen, the place counter, the count and the name tags. Provisional, same page.
- Time trial (K11): Menu > Time trial or the results panel. Your marble alone with a see-through ghost of your best run of any kind. The HUD shows the gap to the ghost. Each trial pays 5 coins. Your best time trial shows in Records.
- Stars (G4.18): each race can earn three: finish, finish in the top three and finish in under 1:00.0. Best stars show on the results, in Records and in the slot list. Stars never lock anything.
- Name tags over the computer marbles, shown only up close.
- Menu: Race and Time trial replace Restart race.

## Judgment calls

- I shipped my recommended music and font now, so the game has real sound right away. BC's picks on the decision page replace them in the next release.
- Art through Gemini and Meshy waits for BC's okay on the art direction (decision page). This release keeps the shapes drawn in code.
- The ghost is your best run of any kind, race or time trial, not only time trials.
