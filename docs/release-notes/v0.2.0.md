# Marble Rush v0.2.0

The first playable build: the gray box and the race in one release. One full track through the toy room, eight marbles, a gate, steering, hop, a short boost, results with coins and six marbles to own.

## Five minute test

1. Play, then slot 1. Behind the gate, steer left and right: your marble (the one with the little cone over it) shuffles. Count 3, 2, 1, the gate drops.
2. Steer down the desk ramp and through the first banked curve. Hold the inside line.
3. Boost on a straight (Shift, X on a controller, or the Boost button). The yellow bar empties and fills again over ten seconds.
4. On the rug the track splits. Try the short left side with the blue bumper posts. Hop over one (Space, A or Hop).
5. Finish into the bucket. Results show your place, time, best time and coins. Race again.
6. Menu > Marbles: buy Sky for 40 coins after a couple of races.
7. Settings > Difficulty: try Easy and Hard. Easy puts rails on every edge.

## New in this version

- One track in four parts, built from data: Desk Drop, Book Stairs, Rug Run and Block Tower. About a minute top to bottom.
- Eight marbles race at once. You steer one. Seven computer marbles race you: Pebble, Comet, Swirl, Tango, Biscuit, Moss and Nova.
- Steering that feels like a marble: light sideways push, gravity does the speed. The inside of a curve is shorter.
- Hop over bumpers and out of crowds.
- Boost (BC, 3:08 PM): a half-second push forward, then a ten-second cooldown (BC, 3:15 PM: short, with a cooldown, no big advantage). In bot races it saves about 7 percent over a whole race. Computer marbles boost too on Normal and Hard, only on straights with rails.
- Some edges have no rail. Roll off and you drop back at the last checkpoint after a second. Easy drops you back 4 m from where you fell.
- The rug split: a short left side with bumper posts or a smooth right side that counts as longer.
- Results: place, time, best time and coins. Coins every race, even last place.
- Menu > Marbles: Ruby is free. Sky, Lime, Sunset, Midnight and Pearl cost 40 to 150 coins. Cosmetic only.
- Easy, Normal and Hard. Tilt to steer on phones (Settings).
- Ten achievements and stats: races, wins, best time, falls.
- A start screen of its own: a toy room corner with a marble pack rolling from a desk to a bucket.
- An app icon of its own: a red glass marble on a striped track.
- Sounds made in code: glass clacks, the gate, a finish fanfare and a rolling sound that follows your speed.

## Judgment calls

- 0.1 (gray box) and 0.2 (the race) shipped as one release, since BC asked for playable.
- Track space physics in place of Jolt rigid bodies (plan said RigidBody3D). The marbles roll on the track as data, so nothing snags on seams and eight marbles cost almost nothing on a phone. A loop-the-loop piece is not possible this way. The Block Tower is a spiral.
- Maple became Moss (green) because a red Maple looked too much like your Ruby.

## Full test checklist

1. `godot --headless --path . -- --check`: DAYONE check ok.
2. `godot --headless --path . -s res://tests/race_check.gd`: RACE check ok. Whole bot races on Easy, Normal and Hard. Every marble reaches the bucket. Winning times about 57 to 60 seconds. The bot wins Easy by about 8 seconds and places 1st to 5th on Normal and Hard.
3. `python3 tests/web_day_one.py`: errors []. Pretend Xbox controller from the start screen into play, Menu, upright.
4. `python3 tests/web_race.py`: errors []. Gate shuffle, the drop, X boost, each area, the finish, results, Race again, Marbles.
5. `tests/common/web_wide_check.py` (WIDE check ok), `review_game_check.py` (errors []), COMMON, SECRETS, SAVESTORE, BUILD check ok, SIGN ok.

## Not tested

A real phone, real touch and tilt, a real Xbox controller and sound levels by ear. The test browser renders at 1 to 4 frames a second, so frame rate on a phone is untested. Music is still the starter stand-in.
