# Marble Rush v0.7.0

A new look for the track and its rails, matched to the app icon you picked. The race plays exactly as before.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over 0.6.1. Your save slots, coins and records stay.
2. Play the Toy Room. Desk Drop and Book Stairs are wood now, with soft grain running down the track.
3. Rug Run is a woven rug. Look for a faint line across the rug every few meters. It is a known seam, fixed in the next release.
4. The rails are rounded, glossy red and cream blocks with small gaps between them. The split on the rug has a rounded divider.
5. Marbles now cast shadows on the track. Check your marble still stands out everywhere.
6. Play the Kitchen. Its rails are the new rounded ones. Its floor looks the same as before. It gets its own surfaces with its painted walls next.
7. Watch the frame rate. Tell me if the first second of a race stutters in the browser.

## Judgment calls

- The wood grain is drawn in code. The art model refused a plain wood texture twice (a false copyright block). The rug weave is painted with Gemini.
- The wood reads a little more orange than the pale wood on the icon. It is one color per area. Say "paler" and I change it.
- The old track floor was lit from behind, so it always looked flat. The new wood and rug face the sun. I toned them down to about the old brightness.
- The rails are a little wider than before (22 cm, was 12 cm). Their inner face, the part a marble touches, did not move.
- Fresh review (G7.14): no must fix, one should fix. The rug texture has a faint seam every 4 m. I left it for 0.8.0 to ship this today. Five notes, the useful ones are in the backlog.
- The Kitchen moves ahead on your word, before your phone notes on the Toy Room walls (G4.13).
- Built in the Chrome side panel chat. G0.18 puts releases in the desktop app.
