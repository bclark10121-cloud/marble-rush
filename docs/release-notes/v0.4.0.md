# Marble Rush v0.4.0

Your decision page picks plus the Kitchen, a second track.

## Five minute test

1. Play. The title is in Luckiest Guy. Quirky Dog plays on the start screen.
2. Menu > Tracks > Kitchen. Eight marbles line up on the counter top.
3. Race the Kitchen: down the counter, a slalom through plate posts in the Dish Rack, S curves across the Table Run with an open stretch, then a spiral down inside the Mixing Bowl.
4. Finish. The time star in the Kitchen is under 54 seconds.
5. Menu > Tracks shows each track's stars and best time. Switch back to the Toy Room: its records are still there.
6. Time trial in the Kitchen keeps its own ghost.

- Your picks (4:13 PM): start screen music Quirky Dog, race music Hyperfun, title font Luckiest Guy, art direction clean toy render, keep Marble Rush with no backup name, next track Kitchen.
