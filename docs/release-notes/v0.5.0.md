# Marble Rush v0.5.0

Your full set of look and sound picks from the decision page.

## Five minute test

1. Look at the app icon on your phone: the Racing pack, three marbles racing down a yellow ramp, your Galaxy marble in front.
2. Open the game. Life of Riley plays on the start screen. The pack on the start screen is led by your Galaxy marble.
3. Play. Glitter Blast plays during the race. Your marble is Galaxy: deep blue with pink and blue rings and little stars.
4. Listen for the wooden toy sounds: knocks on bumpers and rails, a wooden thunk when the gate drops, a xylophone run at the finish, soft wooden clicks in the menus.
5. Menu > Marbles. Galaxy is rolling. Ruby, your old red marble, is still yours for free.
6. Menu > Tracks > Kitchen. Race it once to check the new sounds there too.

## Judgment calls

- The icon is the Racing pack you picked, with your Galaxy marble in the lead instead of the red one. Red was your marble when I drew the sketch.
- Galaxy replaces Ruby as the free starter marble. Ruby stays free in every save, so nobody loses a marble.
- The icon and the Galaxy look are drawn in code. The clean toy render look pass through Gemini and Meshy repaints them later.
- The wooden toy sounds are made in code by `tools/make_marble_sfx.py`, matching the reel on the decision page.
