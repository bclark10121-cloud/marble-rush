## Made for this game

- Wooden toy sounds (knocks, the gate, the finish run, menu clicks and the rolling loop) made by `tools/make_marble_sfx.py`.
- The app icon up to v0.6.0 (Racing pack), drawn in code.
- The four Toy Room wall paintings (window seat, cubby shelves, bunk bed with a slide, chalkboard), made for this game on 2026-11-22 with Google Gemini image models (Nano Banana Pro, through `tools/nano_banana.py` in game-studio, G8.8) from original prompts and BC's picked drafts. No real brands, characters or people (G8.4). Sources in `art_src/nb/bg_walls_final/`, game textures in `art/walls/` made by `tools/make_walls.py`.
- The track textures (v0.7.0): the rug weave made for this game on 2026-11-22 with Google Gemini (Nano Banana 2.1, through the Nano Banana art workflow in game-studio, G8.8) from an original prompt, source `art_src/nb/track/tex_rug.jpg`. The wood grain is drawn in code. Both made into `art/track/` by `tools/make_track_tex.py`.
- The APK icon (v0.6.1), Toy Room run, made for this game on 2026-11-22 with Google Gemini (Nano Banana Pro, G8.8) from original prompts and BC's picked draft. Source `art_src/nb/icon_final/icon_toyroom_run_fit.jpg`.
